\begin{center}
    {\LARGE \heiti 基于多模态数据融合与AI决策的个性化健康管理平台}\\[0.8cm]
    {\large 王彬桥}\\[0.3cm]
    {\normalsize 西南大学计算机与信息科学学院\enspace 软件学院，重庆\enspace 400715}\\[1cm]
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{摘\quad 要：}随着慢性病发病率的持续上升，传统的"治已病"模式已难以满足精准医疗的需求。本研究旨在构建一个基于多模态数据融合的个性化健康管理平台，实现从被动治疗到主动预防的转变。

本系统创新性地融合了基因组学数据（GWAS Catalog）、临床体检数据（NHANES 2017-2020）与行为生活方式数据（MobileWell/IoT），通过构建多基因风险评分（PRS）模型量化先天风险，利用LightGBM算法训练覆盖40种以上慢性病风险目标的预测模型集群，并设计临床-基因-行为三维贝叶斯融合算法实现多模态概率融合。针对异构数据缺乏统一标注的问题，提出了基于领域知识的半监督数据增强策略（表型反推法）。

在系统架构上，平台采用前后端分离与面向服务架构（SOA），通过容器化编排实现一键部署，集成智能OCR体检报告解析、基于RAG的健康问答、AI营养师等功能模块。消融实验结果表明，多模态融合策略相比单模态基线显著提升了风险预测的准确性，验证了本方法的可行性与应用价值。

\vspace{0.5cm}
\noindent\textbf{关键词：}多模态数据融合；贝叶斯推理；多基因风险评分；面向服务架构；个性化健康管理

\thispagestyle{plain}
\begin{center}
    {\LARGE \textbf{Personalized Health Management Platform Based on Multimodal Data Fusion and AI Decision-Making}}\\[0.8cm]
    {\large Wang Binqiao}\\[0.3cm]
    {\normalsize School of Computer and Information Science \& Software, Southwest University, Chongqing 400715, PR China}\\[1cm]
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Abstract:} With the continuous rise in chronic disease prevalence, the traditional ``treat existing diseases'' model can no longer meet the demands of precision medicine. This study aims to construct a personalized health management platform based on multimodal data fusion, enabling a shift from passive treatment to proactive prevention.

The system innovatively integrates genomic data (GWAS Catalog), clinical examination data (NHANES 2017-2020), and behavioral lifestyle data (MobileWell/IoT). It quantifies innate risk through a Polygenic Risk Score (PRS) model, trains a LightGBM model cluster covering over 40 chronic disease risk targets, and designs a clinical-genomic-behavioral three-dimensional Bayesian fusion algorithm for multimodal probabilistic fusion. To address the lack of unified annotation across heterogeneous data, a Domain-Knowledge Guided Semi-supervised Data Augmentation strategy (Phenotype Back-propagation) is proposed.

Architecturally, the platform adopts a front-end/back-end separation with Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA), enabling one-click deployment through containerized orchestration and integrating functional modules including intelligent OCR medical report parsing, RAG-based health Q\&A, and an AI nutritionist. Ablation experiment results demonstrate that the multimodal fusion strategy significantly improves risk prediction accuracy compared to single-modal baselines, validating the feasibility and application value of the proposed approach.

\vspace{0.5cm}
\noindent\textbf{Key words:} Multimodal Data Fusion; Bayesian Inference; Polygenic Risk Score; Service-Oriented Architecture; Personalized Health Management
