\begin{center}
    {\LARGE \heiti 基于多模态数据融合与AI决策的个性化健康管理平台}\\[0.8cm]
    {\large 王彬桥}\\[0.3cm]
    {\normalsize 西南大学计算机与信息科学学院\enspace 软件学院，重庆\enspace 400715}\\[1cm]
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{摘\quad 要：}随着慢性病负担持续上升，传统以“治已病”为主的健康服务模式已难以满足个体化、连续化和前瞻性健康管理需求。本文面向主动健康管理场景，研究并实现了一个基于多模态数据融合与 AI 决策的个性化健康管理平台，旨在打通健康感知、风险评估、干预推演、医疗问答治理与行为验证等关键环节，推动健康管理由被动治疗向主动预防转变。

在方法层面，本文围绕结构化体检指标、基因信息、OCR 医疗文档、IoT 时序观测和医学知识文本等异构来源构建统一证据入口，融合 GWAS Catalog、NHANES 2017--2020 以及 MobileWell/IoT 等数据资源，建立多基因风险评分与临床--基因--行为三维贝叶斯融合风险引擎，并训练覆盖多种慢病风险目标的预测模型集群。针对异构数据监督稀缺与语义不一致问题，本文提出基于领域知识的半监督数据增强策略；进一步地，引入反事实推理机制，对体重、饮食和活动等可干预变量进行情景推演，使系统由静态风险识别扩展到面向干预收益评估的决策支持。

在系统实现与验证层面，平台采用前后端分离和容器化部署方式，构建了融合 OCR 报告解析、检索增强医疗问答与健康建议生成的服务化原型；同时，针对医疗问答场景设计了包含六车道意图分流、工具白名单约束、证据门控、终端策略评估、责任审计与答案回放的 Agent 治理链。实验结果表明，多模态融合策略能够有效提升风险预测性能；系统功能测试与 Harness 行为评测进一步验证了该平台在主动健康管理场景下具备可运行、可治理和可回归验证的原型能力。

\vspace{0.5cm}
\noindent\textbf{关键词：}多模态数据融合；贝叶斯推理；多基因风险评分；反事实推理；医疗Agent治理；个性化健康管理

\begin{center}
    {\LARGE \textbf{Personalized Health Management Platform Based on Multimodal Data Fusion and AI Decision-Making}}\\[0.8cm]
    {\large Wang Binqiao}\\[0.3cm]
    {\normalsize School of Computer and Information Science \& Software, Southwest University, Chongqing 400715, PR China}\\[1cm]
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Abstract:} With the growing burden of chronic diseases, treatment-oriented health services are no longer sufficient for individualized and proactive health management. This thesis develops a personalized health management platform based on multimodal data fusion and AI-driven decision-making, aiming to connect health sensing, risk assessment, intervention simulation, medical dialogue governance, and behavioral validation within a unified framework.

The platform organizes heterogeneous evidence from structured examination indicators, genomic information, OCR-derived medical documents, IoT time-series observations, and medical knowledge texts. By integrating resources such as GWAS Catalog, NHANES 2017--2020, and MobileWell/IoT, the study constructs a Polygenic Risk Score (PRS) module, a clinical-genomic-behavioral Bayesian fusion engine, and a model cluster for chronic disease risk prediction. To address label scarcity and semantic inconsistency across modalities, a domain-knowledge-guided semi-supervised augmentation strategy is introduced. A counterfactual reasoning mechanism is further designed to simulate interventions on controllable variables such as body weight, diet, and physical activity, extending the system from static risk identification to intervention-oriented decision support.

At the system level, the platform adopts a front-end/back-end separated and containerized architecture, and implements a governance pipeline for medical dialogue agents, including six-lane intent routing, tool whitelist constraints, evidence gating, terminal policy evaluation, responsibility auditing, and answer replay. Experimental results indicate that multimodal fusion improves risk prediction performance, while system functional testing and harness-based behavioral evaluation further show that the platform provides a runnable, governable, and regression-verifiable prototype for proactive health management.

\vspace{0.5cm}
\noindent\textbf{Key words:} Multimodal Data Fusion; Bayesian Inference; Polygenic Risk Score; Counterfactual Reasoning; Medical Agent Governance; Personalized Health Management
